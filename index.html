PIP rates 2026/27 (Personal Independence Payment rates)

PIP has four 2026/27 weekly rates. Daily Living standard is £76.70 (8 points), enhanced £114.60 (12 points). Mobility standard is £30.30 (8 points), enhanced £80.00 (12 points). DWP pays every 4 weeks. Uprated 3.8% from 6 April 2026. Source: DWP.

What changed for 2026/27

Policy change · 1 July 2025The 4-point rule was removed

The proposed 4-point rule (which would have required claimants to score at least 4 points on a single Daily Living activity to qualify) was REMOVED from the Bill before enactment, after a Commons rebellion led by Labour backbenchers. Your award is calculated under unchanged 2026/27 rules.

Read the policy note.

Policy change · 3 September 2025Universal Credit Act 2025 enacted

The Act split the UC health element into a protected legacy rate (£429.80/month in 2026/27) for existing LCWRA claimants and a lower rate (£217.26/month) for new claims from 6 April 2026. PIP rates and descriptors are unchanged.

UC health-element split.

Policy change · Ongoing 2026Timms Review under way

The Timms Review of PIP, led by Sir Stephen Timms, is co-produced with disabled people. Scope covers descriptors, assessment, and award reviews. No recommendations have been enacted yet; check gov.uk before relying on any expected change.

Common PIP rate questions

How much is PIP per month in 2026/27?
PIP is paid every 4 weeks, not calendar-monthly. Using the ONS calendar-month factor (weekly rate multiplied by 4.345): Daily Living standard is about £333.26 a month, enhanced about £497.94. Mobility standard is about £131.65, enhanced about £347.60. Enhanced on both components is about £845.54 a month.
How much is standard-rate mobility PIP?
The standard rate of the PIP Mobility component is £30.30 a week in 2026/27 (that is £121.20 every 4 weeks). It is paid when you score 8 to 11 points on the two Mobility activities; 12 or more points pays the enhanced rate of £80.00 a week.
What is the maximum PIP you can get a week?
The maximum is the enhanced rate on both components: £114.60 Daily Living plus £80.00 Mobility, a total of £194.60 a week, or £10,119.20 a year. PIP is tax free.

If you are preparing for a PIP assessmentPrint or screenshot this card

Take a printed copy of these four rates to your Citizens Advice or welfare-rights adviser. The numbers above are the 2026/27 statutory rates; what you receive depends on the points you score on the 10 Daily Living and 2 Mobility activities.

The reliability test applies to every descriptor. You must be able to do an activity safely, to an acceptable standard, repeatedly, and in a reasonable time. If you can do it only on a good day, you cannot do it reliably.
